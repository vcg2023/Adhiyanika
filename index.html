the beginning

Our story

At Adhiyanika, we started with a vision to create unique and stylish clothing for babies and children. Our founder, a mother herself, saw a need for clothing that was both practical and fashionable at the same time. With this in mind, Adhiyanika was born.

Today that same care extends to every hand-finished hair accessory we make — small, wearable pieces of the same craft.

Our Mission

High-quality, stylish, comfortable clothing and accessories that build a child's individuality and confidence — with an experience parents enjoy too.

Our Values

Quality, style, and comfort come first, in clothing and in every stitched accessory — made for diverse tastes and occasions.

Our Expertise

Curating trendy, well-made designs for children and women, and hand-finishing accessories with the same care, piece by piece.
